#### Reaching me

Issues and discussions on the relevant repo work best. You can also find me on the Open WebUI Discord.

#### Working with me

**1. I write very directly.** Short and blunt answers are complete answers and I do not mean them to be degrading in any way. A link is an answer. If I were actually annoyed you would not have to guess.

**2. My time runs on clarity.**
 - You want to report a bug? Exact reproduction steps, full configs, complete logs, the failing command and code references will get my attention.
 - You want to ask a question or propose something? Tell me what you are trying to achieve and why. Often there are other ways of getting there.
 - Urgency, volume, repetition and the wrong tone push your inquiry to the end of the list.

**3. Closing is a triage state.** I close fast: intended behavior, missing repro, wrong venue. Closed issues stay readable, stay searchable and can be reopened the moment new data lands.

None of this is personal and I am happy to assist. All of it is about capacity.
